About Tiến Lên Nova

Tiến Lên Nova is a free online version of Tiến Lên Miền Nam, the classic Vietnamese card game, made so that anyone can play it properly on any device, alone or with the people they care about.

Why we made it

Tiến Lên is a game people learn from their families, and it is at its best when played together. Families today are often spread across cities and countries, and many online versions of the game are crowded with advertising, confusing rules or pressure to spend money. Tiến Lên Nova aims to be the opposite: the real southern rules, clear explanations, computer players that play fairly, and online tables that make it easy to sit down with relatives and friends wherever they are.

What we care about

  • Getting the rules right. The game follows the widely played southern rules, explained openly in our rules guide, and lets each table choose common house rules.
  • Fair play. The cards are shuffled with the device's secure random number generator, and computer players only use information a human player could have.
  • Play money only. Chips have no cash value and cannot be bought or exchanged. The game is for fun, never for gambling.
  • Works everywhere. Phones, tablets, computers and big screens, in Vietnamese and English, with nothing to install.
  • Respect for your data. We keep only what the game needs, and you can delete your account at any time.

Who makes it

Tiến Lên Nova is developed and run by a small independent developer. The game is updated regularly based on feedback from players. If you have a question, a suggestion or a problem, we would like to hear from you on the contact page.

How the site is paid for

The game is free. To cover the cost of running it, this website may show advertising. Ads never affect the cards you receive or how the game is played.
