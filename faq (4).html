Questions and answers

About the game

Is Tiến Lên Nova free?

Yes. The game is free to play on every device. You do not need to pay for anything to play, to get chips, or to play with friends.

Is this gambling? Can I win money?

No. Tiến Lên Nova is a card game for entertainment. Scores are shown as chips, but chips are play money with no cash value. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged for money or prizes, and there is no way to win anything of real value. If you run out, you get free chips again.

Who can play?

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Which rules does the game use?

The southern rules, Tiến Lên Miền Nam, explained in full in our rules guide. Several house rules can be switched on or off, and online the host of a table chooses them for everyone.

Do the computer players cheat?

No. Computer players follow exactly the same rules you do and only know what any player could know: their own cards and the cards already played. They never see your hand. The Hard level remembers which cards have been played, just as a careful human player would.

Playing

Do I need an account?

No. Press "Play now" to play straight away as a guest. A free account (with Google or an email address) keeps your chips, missions and profile picture on every device and lets you play at online tables.

How do I play with my family or friends?

Sign in, choose Play online, then Create a table. Copy the invite link and send it by message, Zalo, Messenger or email. Up to four people can sit at a table; empty seats are filled by computer players.

Which devices work?

Any recent phone, tablet or computer with a web browser: iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, Windows, Mac, Chromebook, and even large TVs and 4K or 8K monitors. Nothing needs to be installed.

Can I add it to my home screen?

Yes. On Android, the game offers an "Add to home screen" button. On iPhone and iPad, open the game in Safari, tap the Share button and choose "Add to Home Screen". The game then opens full screen like an app.

Can I play in Vietnamese or English?

Both. Use the language button in the game, and the link at the top of this website, to switch.

What happens if my internet connection drops during an online hand?

The table keeps going. When you come back to the table with the same account, on the same or another device, you return to your seat with your chips.

Account and privacy

What data do you keep?

Only what the game needs: your account name, picture choice, chips and game progress. The full details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms.

How do I delete my account?

In the game, open Profile and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and its data. Instructions are also in the Privacy Policy.

Help

I found a bug or have an idea. How do I tell you?

Use the contact form, or Help → Feedback inside the game. Bug reports with a short description of what happened are especially helpful.
